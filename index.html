    At the florist bench

    Grow. Wrap. Sell.

    Every bouquet starts as a seed in your garden. When it blooms, bring it to your bench, pick the stems, cut the paper and tie the ribbon. Then put it on the shelf and see who walks in.

    Bouquet of the Day

    A new bouquet, every day

    I wrap a new bouquet in the game each day and share it. This is the collection so far.

    New ones go up daily on X · Instagram · TikTok · Discord

    01

    Make Your Own Bouquets

    Listen to your customer, pick your flowers and make the bouquet with your own hands. The finishing touch is always yours.

    02

    Grow Your Flowers

    Plant your seeds, water them and harvest. Stock your flowers in the flower fridge. Unlock new flowers as your shop grows.

    03

    Fill Your Shelves

    Line your shelves with potted flowers and vases, and get your shop ready for the day.

    04

    Welcome Your Customers

    Every customer has a story: some want flowers for someone they love, others for a birthday. Listen to them and make the bouquet that fits best.

    05

    Ship Your Orders

    If you like, take orders online, pack your bouquets and ship them out.

    06

    Complete Your Album

    Take photos of your bouquets and add them to your album. Fill the recipe pages and keep your favorite shots.

    Hire a Helper

    Tired of waiting at the counter? Hire an employee. Let them handle the sales while you spend more time on your bouquets.

    Play with Friends (Coming Soon)

    Play alone or with up to 4 friends. Run the shop together and grow it side by side.

    Play at Your Own Pace

    • You decide which days your shop opens
    • Just make bouquets, or run the whole shop
    • No time pressure, no penalties
