At the florist bench

Grow. Wrap. Sell.

Every bouquet starts as a seed in your garden. When it blooms, bring it to your bench, pick the stems, cut the paper and tie the ribbon. Then put it on the shelf and see who walks in.

Bouquet of the Day

A new bouquet, every day

I wrap a new bouquet in the game each day and share it. This is the collection so far.

New ones go up daily on X · Instagram · TikTok · Discord

01

Grow your garden

Plant seeds and watch them go from sprout to bloom. Unlock 45 flowers across 15 shop levels, from tulips and roses to peonies and dahlias.

02

Run your shop

Stock the shelves, serve customers who each want something different, ship online orders and decorate the place your way.

03

Wrap it by hand

Pick your stems, cut the paper and tie the ribbon yourself. Paper, vases, pots, boxes or baskets: every bouquet is your own design.

04

Fill your photo album

Snap your favorite bouquets and pin them into a scrapbook. Complete 45 recipe pages, then arrange your best shots on free pages.
