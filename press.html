Short description

A cozy first-person florist game. Grow flowers in your garden, wrap bouquets by hand at your own bench, and run a little flower shop. Unlock 45 flowers, serve customers with special wishes and fill your photo album.

About the game

Panda Flora is a cozy first-person flower shop game. You grow your own flowers, craft every bouquet by hand at your florist bench, and run a little shop that grows with you. Pick the stems, cut the wrapping paper and tie the ribbon yourself, or arrange flowers in vases, pots, boxes and baskets. Customers each want something different, internet orders need shipping, and every bouquet you are proud of can be photographed and pinned into a scrapbook album.

Features

  • Hand-wrapped bouquets: choose flowers, paper and ribbon, every one your own design
  • 45 flowers to grow from seed, unlocked across 15 shop levels
  • A shop to stock, decorate and expand, with walk-in customers and online orders
  • A photo album with 45 recipe pages and free scrapbook pages
  • In active development, with online co-op planned
