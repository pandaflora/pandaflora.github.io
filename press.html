    About the game

    Panda Flora is a cozy game where you run your own flower shop. Plant and grow flowers in your garden, make bouquets to match your customers' wishes, fill your shelves and grow your shop step by step. Every customer has a story: some want flowers for someone they love, others for a birthday. Listen to them, pick your flowers and make the bouquet with your own hands. Take online orders and ship them, photograph your bouquets for your album, and hire a helper when you would rather spend your time at the bench. There is no time pressure and no penalties: you decide which days your shop opens.

    Features

    • Make Your Own Bouquets: Listen to your customer, pick your flowers and make the bouquet with your own hands. The finishing touch is always yours.
    • Grow Your Flowers: Plant your seeds, water them and harvest. Stock your flowers in the flower fridge. Unlock new flowers as your shop grows.
    • Fill Your Shelves: Line your shelves with potted flowers and vases, and get your shop ready for the day.
    • Welcome Your Customers: Every customer has a story: some want flowers for someone they love, others for a birthday. Listen to them and make the bouquet that fits best.
    • Ship Your Orders: If you like, take orders online, pack your bouquets and ship them out.
    • Complete Your Album: Take photos of your bouquets and add them to your album. Fill the recipe pages and keep your favorite shots.
    • Hire a Helper: Tired of waiting at the counter? Hire an employee. Let them handle the sales while you spend more time on your bouquets.
    • Play at Your Own Pace: No time pressure, no penalties
    • In active development, with online co-op planned
